Paper Title: A Suite of Routing Algorithms for Bursty Demands in Quantum Networks

Authors: Anoop Kumar Pandey, Anubhav Srivastava, Pranav K Nayak, Bheemarjuna Reddy Tamma, M V Panduranga Rao

Type: Colab/Jupyter Notebook (ipynb)

Description: Performance comparison of the proposed heuristics and the naive approach under varying network 
and demand parameters: (a) number of demands, (b) EPR pairs per demand, (c) fraction of starved demands, 
(d) network density, (e) burst duration, and (f) average number of spatial splits. Performance comparison for
clustered and scattered demand arrivals.

Submitted to SN Quantum Information Processing.

Instructions: Can be run by uploading to Google Colab or Jupyter Notebook. For LP, CPLEX is required.
